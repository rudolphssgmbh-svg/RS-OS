## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the official registry of normative Engineering documents.

Its purpose is to distinguish normative engineering standards from historical engineering analysis documents.

This document introduces no new architecture.

---

## 2. Engineering Document Categories

Engineering documents are classified into:

- Standards
- Analyses
- Roadmaps
- Runtime References
- Historical Records

Only Engineering Standards define mandatory engineering behavior.

---

## 3. Engineering Standards

The official Engineering Standards are:

- ENG-001 Architecture Compliance Check
- ENG-002 Engineering Review Protocol
- ENG-003 Engineering Approval Workflow
- ENG-004 Engineering Change Classification
- ENG-005 Engineering Evidence Requirements
- ENG-006 Engineering Release Readiness
- ENG-007 Engineering Reference Workflow

These documents form the mandatory engineering baseline.

---

## 4. Historical Engineering Documents

Historical engineering documents include, for example:

- Runtime Route Classification
- Defense Route Analysis
- Runtime Dependency Maps
- Runtime Modularization Strategy
- Dashboard Extraction Plans
- Verification Protocols

These documents provide engineering knowledge but do not define governance rules.

---

## 5. Document Authority

Authority order:

Foundation
→ Architecture
→ Governance
→ Engineering Standards
→ Runtime Documentation
→ Historical Engineering Documents

Lower layers may not redefine higher layers.

---

## 6. Future Engineering Standards

Future normative standards shall continue sequential numbering:

ENG-009
ENG-010
ENG-011
...

Existing historical document identifiers remain unchanged for repository stability.

---

## 7. Registry Maintenance

Whenever a new Engineering Standard is accepted:

- update this registry
- update cross references
- verify document links
- preserve audit history

---

## 8. Final Decision

Decision:

The Engineering Document Registry becomes the official reference index for all normative Engineering Standards.

Constraint:

Only documents listed as Engineering Standards in this registry define mandatory engineering behavior.
